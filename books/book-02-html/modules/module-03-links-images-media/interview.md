# Interview Questions — Links, Images & Media

## Beginner
- What's the difference between a relative and an absolute path?
- What does the `alt` attribute on `<img>` do, and why does it matter?
- What does `target="_blank"` do?
- What's the difference between `<audio>` and `<video>`?

## Intermediate
- Why should `target="_blank"` be paired with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`?
- What's the difference between `srcset` and `<picture>`?
- Why should you provide multiple `<source>` formats for video/audio?
- What's the purpose of `<figure>` and `<figcaption>`?

## Advanced
- Explain the "tabnabbing" vulnerability and how to prevent it.
- Walk through how the browser decides which image to load when using `srcset` and `sizes`.
- What does the `sandbox` attribute on an `<iframe>` do, and why might a site block itself from being iframed elsewhere?
- Why does `alt=""` (empty but present) differ from omitting the `alt` attribute entirely?
