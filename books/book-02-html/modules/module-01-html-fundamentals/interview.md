# Interview Questions — HTML Fundamentals

## Beginner
- What is HTML, and what is it NOT responsible for?
- What's the difference between a tag and an element?
- What is a void (self-closing) element? Give two examples.
- What's the difference between `<head>` and `<body>`?

## Intermediate
- What's the difference between `class` and `id`?
- What's the difference between a block-level and an inline element?
- Why does `<div>` have "no semantic meaning," and why does that matter?
- What does `<meta charset="UTF-8">` do, and why is it important?

## Advanced
- Explain what happens in the browser if you omit `<!DOCTYPE html>`.
- Why might overusing `<div>` for everything ("div soup") be considered bad practice?
- What is the `data-*` attribute pattern used for, and why is it preferable to inventing a custom non-standard attribute?
- Why can emojis be typed directly into HTML, but `<` cannot?
