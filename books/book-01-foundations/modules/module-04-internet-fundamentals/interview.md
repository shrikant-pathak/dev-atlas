# Interview Questions — Internet Fundamentals

## Beginner
- What is the difference between the Internet and the World Wide Web?
- What is DNS, and why is it necessary?
- What's the difference between HTTP and HTTPS?
- What is an IP address?

## Intermediate
- Explain the DNS lookup process, from browser cache to final resolution.
- What's the difference between a public and a private IP address?
- What are the main HTTP methods, and what does each do?
- What is a port, and why is it needed in addition to an IP address?

## Advanced
- Walk me through everything that happens between typing a URL into a browser and the page appearing on screen. *(Classic — be fluent in this, tying together DNS, TCP/TLS, HTTP, and rendering.)*
- Explain what happens during a TLS handshake.
- What's the difference between client-server and peer-to-peer architecture?
- Why might a website choose to use a CNAME record instead of an A record for a subdomain?
