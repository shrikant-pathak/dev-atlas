# Interview Questions — Problem Solving

## Beginner
- What are the three fundamental building blocks of programming logic?
- What is an algorithm?
- What is pseudocode, and why is it useful?
- What's the difference between a syntax error and a logic error?

## Intermediate
- Walk through how you would approach breaking down an unfamiliar problem before writing any code.
- What makes an algorithm "good," beyond just producing the correct output?
- Describe your general process for debugging a piece of code that isn't working.
- What is a flowchart, and when might you use one over pseudocode, or vice versa?

## Advanced
- Given a problem, describe two different algorithmic approaches to solving it and compare their tradeoffs.
- Why might two algorithms that both produce correct results still be considered meaningfully different in quality?
- Explain "rubber duck debugging" and why articulating a problem out loud often helps solve it.
- How would you debug an intermittent bug that you can't reliably reproduce?
