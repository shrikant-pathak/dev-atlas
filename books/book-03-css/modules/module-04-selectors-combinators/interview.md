# Interview Questions — Selectors & Combinators

## Beginner
- What's the difference between a pseudo-class and a pseudo-element?
- What does `:hover` select?
- What's the difference between the descendant combinator and the child combinator?
- What is the correct order for styling link states, and what does the mnemonic stand for?

## Intermediate
- What CSS property is required for `::before`/`::after` to work, and what happens without it?
- Explain the difference between `[href^="http"]`, `[href$=".pdf"]`, and `[href*="example"]`.
- Calculate the specificity of `.nav > ul li a:hover`.
- Why is `!important` generally discouraged in production codebases?

## Advanced
- Explain why `:not()` is considered a "specificity trap," with a concrete example.
- Walk through how you'd resolve a real specificity conflict without reaching for `!important`.
- Explain the `nth-child(An+B)` formula in detail, with two different example values substituted for A and B.
- Why does combinator choice (descendant vs. child) matter specifically for multi-level dropdown navigation menus?
