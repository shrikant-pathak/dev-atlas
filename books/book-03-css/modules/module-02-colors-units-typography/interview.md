# Interview Questions — Colors, Units & Typography

## Beginner
- What are the four main ways to specify a color in CSS?
- What's the difference between `px` and `%`?
- What is a font stack, and why does it matter?
- What does `line-height` control?

## Intermediate
- What's the difference between `em` and `rem`, and why does that difference matter in nested elements?
- What does the alpha channel in RGBA/HSLA control?
- Why might HSL be more convenient than RGB when creating hover states?
- What are the two main ways to add a custom web font to a project?

## Advanced
- Explain the "compounding" problem with `em` in detail, with a concrete nested example.
- What does `font-display: swap` do, and what problem does it solve?
- Compare icon fonts and SVG icons in terms of accessibility and styling flexibility.
- Why is `vh`/`vw` particularly useful for full-screen hero sections, compared to percentage or pixel units?
