# Interview Questions — The Box Model

## Beginner
- What are the four layers of the CSS box model?
- What's the difference between margin and padding?
- What does `border-radius: 50%` do to a square element?
- What does `list-style-type: none` do?

## Intermediate
- Explain margin collapsing with a concrete example.
- What problem does `box-sizing: border-box` solve, and why is it applied almost universally?
- What's the difference between `outline` and `border` in terms of the box model?
- What's the difference between `object-fit: cover` and `object-fit: contain`?

## Advanced
- Walk through the exact width calculation of an element under `content-box` vs. `border-box`, given specific width/padding/border values.
- Why is removing the default focus outline without a replacement considered a genuine accessibility mistake?
- Explain why tables are notoriously difficult to make responsive, and what module addresses this properly.
- What does the `::marker` pseudo-element let you control that older list-styling properties couldn't?
