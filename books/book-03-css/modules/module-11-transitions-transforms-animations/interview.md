# Module 11 Interview Questions — Transitions, Transforms & Animations

1. What are the four parts of the `transition` shorthand, and which are required?
2. Why can't `display` be meaningfully transitioned?
3. What's the difference between `translate()` and changing `top`/`left` for moving an element?
4. Does the order of chained transform functions matter? Give an example.
5. What does `transform-origin` control?
6. Why is `perspective` required for 3D transforms to render believably?
7. What does `backface-visibility: hidden` do, and why does a flip card need it?
8. What does `transform-style: preserve-3d` allow that isn't possible by default?
9. What's the structural difference between `transition` and `@keyframes`?
10. What does `animation-fill-mode: forwards` do?
11. Why are `transform` and `opacity` preferred for performant animations over properties like `width` or `top`?
12. What's the difference between `scroll-snap-type: mandatory` and `proximity`?
13. Which element does `scroll-snap-align` apply to?
14. What problem does `scroll-padding-top` solve?
15. How would you build a CSS-only loading spinner?
