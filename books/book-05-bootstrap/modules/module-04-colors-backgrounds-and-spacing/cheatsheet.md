# Module 04 Cheatsheet

## Theme Colors
`primary` `secondary` `success` `danger` `warning` `info` `light` `dark`

## Text Color
`.text-{color}` · `.text-body` · `.text-body-secondary` · `.text-muted` · `.text-white`

## Background Color
`.bg-{color}` · `.bg-{color}-subtle` · `.text-{color}-emphasis` (pair with subtle bg)

## Opacity
`.text-opacity-{25|50|75|100}` · `.bg-opacity-{25|50|75|100}`

## Borders
`.border` · `.border-0` · `.border-{top|end|bottom|start}` · `.border-{top|end|bottom|start}-0`
`.border-{color}` · `.border-{color}-subtle` · `.border-{1|2|3|4|5}`

## Rounding
`.rounded` · `.rounded-0` … `.rounded-3` · `.rounded-{top|end|bottom|start}` · `.rounded-circle` · `.rounded-pill`

## Spacing Syntax
`{m|p}{t|b|s|e|x|y|blank}-{0-5|auto}`
- `m` margin, `p` padding
- sides: t/b/s/e/x/y, blank = all sides
- size: 0–5 scale (`.25rem` steps to `3rem`) or `auto` (margin only)
- responsive: `{property}{sides}-{breakpoint}-{size}`

## Sizing
`.w-{25|50|75|100|auto}` · `.h-{25|50|75|100|auto}`
`.vw-100` · `.vh-100` · `.min-vh-100`
`.mw-100` · `.mh-100`
