# Module 03 Interview Questions — Typography & Content

1. What is the practical benefit of using a `.display-*` class on an `<h1>` instead of a custom `font-size`?
2. Why does Bootstrap use `.text-start`/`.text-end` instead of `.text-left`/`.text-right`?
3. What problem does RFS (Responsive Font Sizes) solve, and how does its approach differ from writing your own font-size media queries?
4. Which Bootstrap classes have RFS applied to them automatically by default?
5. Is RFS something you apply as an HTML class, or is it a Sass-level tool? Explain the distinction.
6. What does `.img-fluid` actually do under the hood?
7. Why is `.d-block` required alongside `.mx-auto` to center an image?
8. What's the difference between `.rounded` and `.rounded-circle` on an image?
9. Name at least three table modifier classes and what each does.
10. What is the difference between `.table-responsive` and `.table-responsive-md`?
11. Does Bootstrap's table styling remove the need for `scope="col"`/`scope="row"` attributes? Why or why not?
12. What is the purpose of pairing `<pre>` with `<code>` for a multi-line snippet?
13. Why was the dedicated `.jumbotron` component removed in Bootstrap 5, and what replaced it conceptually?
14. Walk through how you'd rebuild a "jumbotron"-style hero section using only utility classes.
15. What role does a nested `.container` play inside an outer `.container-fluid` hero section?
